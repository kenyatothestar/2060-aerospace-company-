# 2060 Aerospace Systems

## Company Profile

2060 Aerospace Systems is an early-stage aerospace technology
company focused on building software for the future of space
mission operations.

### Flagship Product

2060 Mission Control V5.0

### What We Are Building

A modern mission-operations platform designed to bring telemetry,
spacecraft systems, communications, navigation and operational
analytics into one software environment.

### Current Stage

Working software prototype and demonstration platform.

### Long-Term Goal

Develop technology products that can serve commercial space
companies, research organizations, universities and other
organizations involved in space missions.

### Mission

Make advanced mission-operations technology more accessible,
efficient and easier to operate.

### Vision

Build 2060 into a globally recognized aerospace technology company.

### Transparency

The current Mission Control telemetry is simulated and intended
for demonstration. Production aerospace use requires extensive
engineering validation, cybersecurity, safety testing and applicable
regulatory approval.
