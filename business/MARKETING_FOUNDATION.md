# 2060 Aerospace Systems — Marketing Foundation

## Positioning

2060 Aerospace Systems is building modern mission-operations
software for the future of space.

## Product

2060 Mission Control

## Primary message

Mission information. Spacecraft systems. Operations.
One unified software environment.

## Audience

- Aerospace startups
- Space technology companies
- Universities
- Research organizations
- Mission simulation teams
- Aerospace engineers

## Content themes

### Product
Demonstrate Mission Control capabilities.

### Education
Explain mission operations, telemetry and spacecraft systems.

### Building in public
Show the progress of the 2060 project honestly.

### Vision
Share the long-term vision for aerospace technology.

### Customer problems
Discuss real operational challenges discovered through interviews.

## Marketing principle

Build credibility through useful information, demonstrations
and real customer evidence rather than exaggerated claims.
