# 2060 First Outreach Campaign

## Campaign objective

Find the first qualified organizations interested in evaluating
Mission Control.

## Target

Build an initial list of 25 relevant organizations.

Suggested distribution:
- 10 aerospace/space technology companies
- 5 universities
- 5 research organizations
- 5 simulation/training organizations

## Campaign process

### Step 1
Research each organization.

### Step 2
Identify an appropriate contact.

### Step 3
Record the organization in the lead tracker.

### Step 4
Send a short personalized introduction.

### Step 5
Ask whether they would be interested in a brief demonstration.

### Step 6
Follow up once if appropriate.

### Step 7
Record the result.

## Success metric

The first campaign succeeds if it produces:
- Qualified conversations
- Demo requests
- Pilot discussions

Do not measure success only by followers or views.

## Rule

No spam. Personalize outreach and respect organizations'
communication preferences.
