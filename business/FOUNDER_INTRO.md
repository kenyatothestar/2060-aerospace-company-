# 2060 Aerospace Systems — Founder Introduction

## Introduction

I am the founder of 2060 Aerospace Systems, an early-stage
technology company focused on building software for future
space mission operations.

I started 2060 with a long-term vision: to build useful
technology that can contribute to the growth of the space
industry.

## What I Have Built

I have developed a working Mission Control software prototype
that demonstrates mission dashboards, telemetry, spacecraft
systems, communications, navigation and analytics.

## What I Am Looking For

I am looking for:
- Early customers
- Pilot users
- Technical collaborators
- Aerospace partnerships
- Mentors
- Investors when the business has sufficient validation

## My Approach

Start small, solve real problems, learn from users and build
the company step by step.

## Transparency

The current Mission Control platform uses simulated telemetry.
It is a prototype and is not certified for real spacecraft
operations.
