# 2060 Aerospace Systems — Legal & Compliance Plan

## Company

Maintain appropriate:
- Company registration
- Ownership records
- Contracts
- Accounting records
- Tax records
- Business licenses where applicable

## Software

Maintain:
- Terms of service
- Privacy documentation
- Software licenses
- Open-source attribution
- Customer agreements

## Customer data

Protect customer information using appropriate:
- Access controls
- Data handling procedures
- Security practices
- Retention policies

## Aerospace considerations

Before commercial aerospace deployment, determine which
laws, regulations, export controls, safety requirements and
industry standards may apply to the specific product,
customer and jurisdiction.

## Contracts

Important agreements may include:
- Pilot agreement
- Service agreement
- NDA
- Software license
- Data-processing terms
- Partnership agreement

## Legal review

Use qualified legal professionals for contracts, regulatory
questions, intellectual property and aerospace compliance.

This document is a planning framework, not legal advice.
