# 2060 Customer Requirements System

## Requirement record

For every customer requirement record:

- Organization
- Contact
- Date
- Problem
- Requested capability
- Current workaround
- Business impact
- Priority
- Number of customers affected
- Status
- Notes

## Requirement types

### Mission Operations
Requests related to mission monitoring and operational workflows.

### Telemetry
Requests related to displaying or analyzing mission data.

### Systems
Requests related to spacecraft-system monitoring.

### Communications
Requests related to communication workflows.

### Analytics
Requests related to reports, trends and decision support.

### Integration
Requests involving external software or data systems.

## Rule

Customer requests must be validated before becoming product
commitments.
