# 2060 Aerospace Systems — Partnership Plan

## Objective

Build relationships that can help 2060 validate its product,
improve its technology and reach potential customers.

## Potential partners

### Universities
For research, education and mission-simulation projects.

### Aerospace startups
For pilot projects and software collaboration.

### Research organizations
For testing ideas and understanding operational requirements.

### Technology companies
For infrastructure, data, cloud and software integrations.

### Aerospace mentors
For industry knowledge, technical guidance and introductions.

## Partnership approach

1. Identify organizations with relevant problems.
2. Research their work before contacting them.
3. Introduce 2060 briefly.
4. Demonstrate the prototype.
5. Ask about their needs.
6. Propose a small pilot or collaboration.
7. Document the relationship.

## Principle

Seek mutually useful partnerships rather than asking for money
before demonstrating value.
