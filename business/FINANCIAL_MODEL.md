# 2060 Aerospace Systems — Initial Financial Model

## Objective

Build a financially sustainable aerospace software company.

## Revenue streams

1. Paid pilot projects
2. Custom software development
3. Mission simulation and training
4. Software subscriptions
5. Enterprise licensing
6. Integration and engineering services

## Initial cost categories

- Hosting
- Software infrastructure
- Domain and business services
- Development tools
- Security
- Legal and accounting
- Marketing and customer acquisition
- Travel and demonstrations when required

## Financial milestones

### Milestone 1
First qualified prospect.

### Milestone 2
First product demonstration.

### Milestone 3
First pilot.

### Milestone 4
First revenue.

### Milestone 5
Recurring customer revenue.

### Milestone 6
Multiple paying customers.

### Milestone 7
Sustainable operating economics.

## Principle

Do not assume billionaire-scale revenue.
Build evidence step by step through customers, revenue and
product-market fit.
