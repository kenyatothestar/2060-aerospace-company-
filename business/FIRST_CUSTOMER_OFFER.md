# 2060 First Customer Offer

## Early Pilot Program

### What we offer

A demonstration and pilot version of 2060 Mission Control
for organizations interested in evaluating mission-monitoring
software.

### Pilot includes

- Mission dashboard
- Simulated telemetry demonstration
- Spacecraft systems monitoring
- Communications interface
- Navigation/trajectory visualization
- Feedback session

### Customer benefit

The pilot allows an organization to evaluate the concept and
identify the mission-monitoring capabilities it would actually
need.

### Commercial approach

Pricing should be agreed after understanding the customer's
requirements, scope and implementation needs.

### Important

The current platform uses simulated telemetry and is not
certified for real spacecraft operations.
