# 2060 Customer Issue Tracker

## Issue fields

- Issue ID
- Date reported
- Organization
- Contact
- Category
- Description
- Severity
- Assigned owner
- Status
- Resolution
- Date resolved
- Customer confirmation

## Status values

- New
- Investigating
- Waiting for customer
- In progress
- Resolved
- Closed

## Severity

### Critical
Customer cannot use a major production capability.

### High
Important workflow significantly affected.

### Medium
Workaround available.

### Low
Minor issue or cosmetic problem.

## Rule

Every customer issue must have an owner and a next action.
