# 2060 Aerospace Systems — Investor Pitch Structure

## 1. Vision

2060 TO THE STARS

Build software infrastructure for the next generation
of space operations.

## 2. Problem

Mission teams can face fragmented operational information,
complex workflows and difficult-to-use software systems.

## 3. Solution

2060 Mission Control provides a unified software environment
for mission monitoring, telemetry visualization, systems,
communications, navigation and analytics.

## 4. Product

Show the working Mission Control prototype and explain the
current capabilities honestly.

## 5. Market

Identify the specific customer segments that have the strongest
need and willingness to pay.

## 6. Business Model

Potential revenue:
- Software subscriptions
- Enterprise licensing
- Pilot projects
- Custom engineering
- Integration services

## 7. Traction

Use only verified evidence:
- Demonstrations
- Customer interviews
- Pilot users
- Paying customers
- Revenue
- Partnerships

## 8. Competitive Advantage

Explain what 2060 can do better, faster or more effectively
than existing alternatives.

## 9. Roadmap

Show the path from prototype → customer validation →
production platform → commercial aerospace software.

## 10. Financials

Present realistic assumptions, costs, revenue projections
and funding requirements.

## 11. Funding

Clearly state:
- Amount sought
- Intended use
- Development milestones
- Expected runway

## 12. Team

Show relevant founder, engineering, business and advisory
capabilities.

## 13. Closing

2060 Aerospace Systems

2060 TO THE STARS

## Rule

Never present simulated results, projections or future plans
as current achievements.
