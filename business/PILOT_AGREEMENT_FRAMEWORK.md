# 2060 Pilot Agreement Framework

## Purpose

Define a small, controlled pilot for evaluating 2060 Mission Control.

## Recommended sections

### 1. Parties
Identify the customer and 2060 Aerospace Systems.

### 2. Scope
Clearly describe what software and services are included.

### 3. Duration
Specify the pilot start and end dates.

### 4. Deliverables
List exactly what will be provided.

### 5. Customer responsibilities
Define access, feedback, testing and required cooperation.

### 6. Fees
State whether the pilot is paid, free, or discounted.

### 7. Data and security
Define what data may be used and how it will be protected.

### 8. Intellectual property
Define ownership of existing software and any custom work.

### 9. Support
Define support hours and response expectations.

### 10. Limitations
The current platform uses simulated telemetry and is not
certified for real spacecraft operations.

### 11. Termination
Define how either party can end the pilot.

### 12. Success criteria
Define measurable outcomes for deciding whether to continue.

## Legal note

This is a business framework, not legal advice. A qualified
lawyer should review any agreement before it is signed.
