# 2060 Customer Outreach Guide

## Goal

Start conversations with potential early users of 2060 Mission Control.

## Who to contact

- Aerospace technology startups
- Universities and engineering departments
- Research organizations
- Mission simulation companies
- Aerospace software teams

## First message structure

1. Introduce yourself and 2060 Aerospace Systems.
2. Explain that you have built a Mission Control software prototype.
3. Ask whether they would be interested in seeing a short demo.
4. Ask about their mission-monitoring or simulation needs.
5. Do not make claims about capabilities the prototype does not have.

## Follow-up

If they respond positively:
- Schedule a demonstration.
- Record their needs.
- Ask what they would pay for or test.
- Record the next action.

## Professional rule

Be honest, concise and respectful.
Do not send mass spam.
