# 2060 Aerospace Systems — One-Page Business Plan

## Problem

Space and aerospace organizations need reliable software for
monitoring mission information and coordinating operations.

## Solution

2060 Mission Control is a unified software interface for
mission monitoring, telemetry, spacecraft systems,
communications, navigation and analytics.

## Current Product

A working Mission Control prototype demonstrating the product
concept using simulated telemetry.

## Initial Customers

- Aerospace startups
- Research organizations
- Universities
- Training and simulation organizations
- Aerospace engineering teams

## Initial Revenue

- Paid pilot projects
- Custom dashboards
- Simulation and training software
- Software development and integration services

## Growth

Start with pilot customers, learn from their requirements,
improve the product, establish recurring revenue and then
expand into larger aerospace markets.

## Long-Term Vision

Build 2060 Aerospace Systems into a global aerospace technology
company.

## Current Stage

Early-stage prototype seeking customer validation.

## Important

Do not represent simulated telemetry as live spacecraft data.
