# 2060 Aerospace Systems — Business Continuity Plan

## Objective

Keep essential business and software operations running during
unexpected disruptions.

## Critical functions

- Customer communication
- Mission Control demonstrations
- Source-code access
- Production service
- Customer support
- Financial records
- Business documentation

## Disruption response

### Software outage

1. Identify the affected service.
2. Follow the incident process.
3. Restore from the latest known-good release.
4. Verify service.
5. Inform affected customers when appropriate.

### Infrastructure failure

1. Identify the failed infrastructure.
2. Activate backup/recovery procedures.
3. Restore essential services.
4. Verify data integrity.
5. Document the event.

### Key-person unavailability

Maintain organized documentation so another authorized person
can understand:
- System architecture
- Deployment process
- Customer status
- Business operations
- Recovery procedures

## Business continuity principle

Protect people, customer information, source code and essential
business operations first.
