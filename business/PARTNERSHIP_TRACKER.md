# 2060 Partnership & Contact Tracker

## Contact record

For every potential partner record:

- Organization
- Contact name
- Role
- Contact method
- Organization type
- Date contacted
- Relationship stage
- Main interest
- Potential value
- Next action
- Follow-up date
- Notes

## Organization types

- Aerospace company
- Space technology startup
- University
- Research organization
- Simulation/training organization
- Technology company
- Investor
- Mentor
- Other

## Relationship stages

1. Identified
2. Researched
3. Contacted
4. Responded
5. Meeting
6. Demonstration
7. Pilot discussion
8. Partnership
9. Not proceeding

## Principle

Build relationships based on mutual value.
Respect privacy and communication preferences.
