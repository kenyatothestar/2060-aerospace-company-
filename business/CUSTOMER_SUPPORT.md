# 2060 Customer Support System

## Support objectives

Provide customers with a clear process for reporting problems,
asking questions and requesting assistance.

## Support categories

### Technical
Software errors, access problems and performance issues.

### Product
Questions about Mission Control capabilities.

### Account
User access and account administration.

### Feature Request
Requests for new capabilities.

### General
Other business or product questions.

## Support process

1. Receive the request.
2. Record the request.
3. Assign a priority.
4. Investigate.
5. Communicate progress.
6. Resolve the issue.
7. Confirm with the customer.
8. Close and document the request.

## Principle

Customer support should be professional, clear and respectful.
Never promise a fix or feature before it has been validated.
