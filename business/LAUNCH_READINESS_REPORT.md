# 2060 Aerospace Systems — Launch Readiness Report

## Current Position

The 2060 Mission Control project has progressed from prototype
development toward a structured software business foundation.

## Readiness areas

### Technology
Mission Control software, backend documentation, architecture,
deployment procedures and technical audit.

### Product
Product roadmap, demo process, customer requirements and
prioritization system.

### Customers
Support, issue tracking, feedback and pilot metrics.

### Sales
Outreach, sales pipeline and partnership tracking.

### Business
Pricing, financial planning, KPIs and 90-day action planning.

### Operations
Monitoring, release management, backup/recovery and continuity.

### Legal
Compliance planning and intellectual-property protection.

### Investor readiness
Pitch structure and organized investor data room.

## Important distinction

The project is software-business ready for continued validation
and customer development only to the extent supported by actual
testing and evidence.

It is NOT automatically certified for real aerospace operations.

## Next objective

Move from documentation and prototype work toward:

Prototype → Customer validation → Pilot → Revenue → Production
