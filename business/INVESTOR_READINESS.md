# 2060 Investor Readiness Checklist

## Company

- [ ] Clear company mission
- [ ] Clear product description
- [ ] Founder story
- [ ] Business model
- [ ] Target market

## Product

- [ ] Working prototype
- [ ] Product demonstration
- [ ] Product roadmap
- [ ] Customer feedback
- [ ] Security plan
- [ ] Production architecture

## Business

- [ ] Customer pipeline
- [ ] First pilot
- [ ] First revenue
- [ ] Revenue model
- [ ] Basic financial model

## Investment

- [ ] Pitch deck
- [ ] One-page company summary
- [ ] Funding requirement
- [ ] Use of funds
- [ ] Milestones
- [ ] Investor data room

## Principle

Investment should support evidence of a real opportunity.
Do not exaggerate traction, revenue or technical capabilities.
