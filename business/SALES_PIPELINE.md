# 2060 Aerospace Systems — Sales Pipeline

## Pipeline stages

### 1. Prospect
Organization identified as a possible customer.

### 2. Contacted
Initial introduction sent.

### 3. Qualified
The organization has a relevant problem and potential interest.

### 4. Demo
Mission Control demonstration completed or scheduled.

### 5. Pilot Discussion
Customer is discussing a potential pilot.

### 6. Proposal
Commercial proposal prepared or submitted.

### 7. Negotiation
Scope, pricing and terms being discussed.

### 8. Won
Customer has agreed to proceed.

### 9. Lost
Opportunity will not proceed.

## Lead information

Record:

- Organization
- Contact
- Contact method
- Date added
- Problem
- Pipeline stage
- Estimated value
- Next action
- Next-action date
- Notes

## Sales principle

Focus on solving customer problems.
Do not make claims about capabilities that the product does not
currently provide.
