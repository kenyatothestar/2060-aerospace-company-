# 2060 Aerospace Systems — First 90 Days

## Days 1–30: Validation

- Identify potential customers.
- Contact relevant organizations.
- Conduct customer interviews.
- Demonstrate Mission Control.
- Record problems and requirements.
- Build a prospect pipeline.

## Days 31–60: Pilot

- Select the strongest potential pilot customer.
- Define the pilot scope.
- Improve the product around validated needs.
- Agree on measurable pilot outcomes.
- Begin pilot testing.

## Days 61–90: Revenue

- Complete the pilot.
- Measure results.
- Prepare a commercial proposal.
- Seek the first paid engagement.
- Document customer feedback.
- Create a repeatable sales process.

## Main target

The primary 90-day goal is not fame or a huge valuation.

The goal is:

FIRST REAL CUSTOMER + FIRST REAL REVENUE.

That provides the foundation for larger growth.
