# 2060 Mission Control — Pricing Strategy

## Pricing principle

Pricing should be based on customer value, implementation scope,
support requirements and actual product capabilities.

## Initial commercial models

### Pilot
A limited evaluation period or project with defined scope.

### Professional
For smaller organizations requiring a hosted mission-monitoring
platform and standard support.

### Enterprise
For organizations requiring customized deployments,
integrations, security controls and dedicated support.

### Custom Engineering
For specialized dashboards, integrations or software development.

## Pricing process

1. Understand the customer's problem.
2. Define the required scope.
3. Estimate implementation and support costs.
4. Agree on measurable pilot outcomes.
5. Present a written proposal.
6. Review results before expanding the contract.

## Important

Do not promise features that do not currently exist.
Final prices should be agreed based on actual scope and market
validation.
