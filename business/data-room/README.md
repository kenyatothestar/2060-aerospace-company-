# 2060 Investor Data Room

## Purpose

Organize important company information for potential investors,
partners and qualified reviewers.

## Sections

### 01 — Company
- Company overview
- Mission
- Founder information
- Company structure

### 02 — Product
- Mission Control overview
- Product roadmap
- Technical architecture
- Product demonstrations

### 03 — Market
- Target customers
- Customer research
- Market assumptions
- Competitive research

### 04 — Business
- Business model
- Pricing strategy
- Financial model
- 90-day plan

### 05 — Customers
- Customer requirements
- Pilot information
- Customer feedback
- Customer success metrics

### 06 — Technology
- API documentation
- Security baseline
- Deployment runbook
- Production readiness

### 07 — Legal
- Company documents
- Contracts
- IP documentation
- Compliance planning

## Security

Private investor and customer documents must not be placed
in a public repository unless intentionally approved.
