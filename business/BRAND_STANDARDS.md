2060 Aerospace Systems

2060 TO THE STARS

## Brand personality

- Professional
- Technical
- Ambitious
- Honest
- Future-focused
- Reliable

## Communication

Use clear language.
Explain technical concepts simply.
Avoid exaggerated aerospace claims.

## Trust principle

Always distinguish:
- Prototype
- Simulation
- Demonstration
- Production system
- Certified aerospace system

Never describe simulated telemetry as real spacecraft telemetry.
