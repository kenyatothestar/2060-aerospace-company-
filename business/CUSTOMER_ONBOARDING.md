# 2060 Customer Onboarding Plan

## Stage 1 — Introduction

- Understand the customer's organization.
- Identify the relevant user.
- Understand the problem they want to solve.

## Stage 2 — Demo

- Demonstrate Mission Control.
- Explain current capabilities.
- Clearly identify simulated components.

## Stage 3 — Pilot Setup

- Define pilot scope.
- Define users.
- Define timeline.
- Define success criteria.
- Confirm responsibilities.

## Stage 4 — Pilot

- Provide access.
- Monitor usage.
- Collect feedback.
- Resolve agreed issues.

## Stage 5 — Review

- Measure results.
- Compare against success criteria.
- Identify improvements.
- Discuss commercial continuation.

## Stage 6 — Expansion

If the pilot succeeds:
- Define expanded scope.
- Agree on pricing.
- Establish support.
- Begin longer-term engagement.
