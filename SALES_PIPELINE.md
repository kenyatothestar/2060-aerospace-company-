# 2060 Sales Pipeline

## Pipeline stages

1. Prospect identified
2. Initial contact
3. Conversation
4. Demo scheduled
5. Demo completed
6. Pilot discussion
7. Proposal
8. Pilot/customer
9. Paid customer
10. Expansion/referral

## Prospect record

For every prospect record:

- Organization
- Contact person
- Contact method
- Problem identified
- Demo status
- Next action
- Follow-up date
- Potential value
- Current stage

## Rule

Do not spam people.

Focus on organizations where Mission Control could genuinely
solve a problem.
