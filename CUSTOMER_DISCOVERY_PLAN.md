# 2060 Customer Discovery Plan

## Objective

Find real people and organizations that have problems our software
could potentially solve.

## First target groups

1. Aerospace and space-technology startups
2. Universities with aerospace or engineering programs
3. Research organizations
4. Mission simulation and training organizations
5. Technology companies interested in aerospace software

## Discovery process

### Step 1
Show the Mission Control prototype.

### Step 2
Ask what mission-monitoring problems they currently experience.

### Step 3
Ask which features would save them time or money.

### Step 4
Ask whether they would test a product like 2060 Mission Control.

### Step 5
Record feedback and identify repeated problems.

### Step 6
Build only the features customers actually need.

## Goal

Obtain the first serious pilot user before attempting large-scale
fundraising.
