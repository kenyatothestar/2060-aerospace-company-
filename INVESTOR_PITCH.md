# 2060 Aerospace Systems — Initial Pitch

## The Opportunity

As space activity expands, mission teams need modern software
for monitoring complex operations and large amounts of mission data.

## Our Solution

2060 Mission Control provides a unified interface for mission
monitoring, telemetry, spacecraft systems, communications,
navigation and analytics.

## What Exists Today

We have built a working Mission Control software prototype that
can demonstrate the product concept.

## Business Direction

Potential customers include:
- Space technology companies
- Research organizations
- Universities and educational programs
- Aerospace engineering teams
- Mission simulation and training organizations

## Business Model

Potential revenue models include:
- Software subscriptions
- Enterprise licensing
- Custom mission-control software
- Training and simulation platforms
- Integration and engineering services

## The Ask

The immediate objective is to validate the market, find early
users and customers, improve the product based on feedback and
build partnerships before pursuing larger investment.

## Current Stage

Early-stage prototype.

## Important

The current telemetry is simulated and the platform is not
certified for real spacecraft operations.
