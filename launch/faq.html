MISSION CONTROL FAQ

What is 2060 Mission Control?

It is a software platform demonstrating a unified interface for mission monitoring and operational information.

Who is it for?

Potential users include aerospace companies, research organizations, universities and mission-simulation teams.

Is the telemetry real?

No. The current telemetry is simulated demonstration data.

Can it control a real spacecraft?

Not currently. The present platform is a prototype and is not certified for real spacecraft operations.

Can organizations request a demo?

Yes. Organizations can contact 2060 Aerospace Systems to discuss a demonstration or potential pilot.
